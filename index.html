What the measures mean

  • Within four hours: the share of A&E attendances where the patient was admitted, transferred or discharged within four hours of arriving.
  • Major A&E (Type 1): consultant-led, 24-hour emergency departments with full resuscitation facilities. All types also includes urgent treatment centres, walk-in centres and minor injury units.
  • 12-hour waits: patients who waited more than 12 hours between the decision to admit them and actually getting a bed.

Targets

  • The NHS Constitution standard is 95% within four hours. England last met it in 2015.
  • For 2026-27, trusts are asked to reach 82% across all A&E types. The longer-term aim is 85% by 2028-29.
  • Status labels in the table compare each provider's all-types figure with the 82% target.

Changes to watch

  • Bristol merger. North Bristol NHS Trust stops reporting after June 2026. From July its activity is included in Bristol NHS Foundation Trust, which explains that trust's jump in attendances.
  • Two Leicestershire urgent care sites report from May 2026 only.
  • Ashford Walk-In-Centre is missing from NHS England's ICB mapping file, so it appears under its region only.
